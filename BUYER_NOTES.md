# Buyer Notes — stdhook-cli

## What this is
A tiny, zero-dependency Python CLI that generates and verifies Standard Webhooks (Svix-compatible) signatures. Perfect for indie developers, solo founders, and teams who need a simple, auditable tool without pulling in heavy libraries.

## Who it's for
- Solo founders integrating webhooks into their micro-SaaS
- Developers who want to test webhook endpoints locally
- Teams standardizing on the Standard Webhooks spec
